# policy

1. No Abuse

Do not spam commands overload the bot on purpose or misuse its features.

2. No Harmful Use

Leafbot should not be used to harm, disrupt or target users or Discord servers.

3. Content Rules

Do not use Leafbot to send harassing, threatening or otherwise harmful content.

4. About Notifications

Game release alerts are provided as-is. Sometimes they may be late. Contain incorrect information.

5. Privacy

Leafbot only keeps the data needed for its functions. We do not store user data that is not necessary.
